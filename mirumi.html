Mirumi — AI Emotion
Tracking Journal

↗ GitHub Repository ↗ Live Demo
3rd Place — CBC AI Builders Hackathon 2026 · IIT Chapter. Users write a short daily journal entry. Claude analyzes the emotional content, tracks mood patterns and relationship dynamics, and generates a clinical briefing for their therapist. No account. No server. Data never leaves the device until the user chooses to share it.
Problem Statement
1 in 3 students experience depression; only 37% get help. At IIT: 1 counselor per 4,250 students. Anxiety among international STEM students up 78% since 2015.
Responsible AI
Crisis alerts require 3 independent signals to converge: mood streak, language pattern (C-SSRS aligned), and relationship score. No single AI call can trigger a flag. Disclaimer always visible: "AI flags are not diagnostic. Clinical responsibility stays with the counselor."
Privacy
No backend. No account. Data lives entirely in the browser until the student chooses to share. One intentional JSON export handed directly to their counselor — nothing automatic, nothing stored on a server.

// How It Works

Three AI Pillars

Journal Analysis
Claude classifies mood, surfaces emotional subtext, and delivers a CBT-style reframe — turning each entry into a therapeutic micro-session.
Relationship Health Score
0–100 score weighted across emotional tone, conflict patterns, and repair signals. Tracks trajectory, not just individual days.
Therapist Briefing
One clinical-quality paragraph synthesizing the week's mood arc, relationship themes, and risk signals — readable in 60 seconds before a session.
